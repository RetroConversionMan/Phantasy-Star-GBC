PHANTASY STAR — NATIVE GAME BOY COLOR PORT

Open Phantasy Star.gbc in a Game Boy Color emulator.
4 MiB ROM, MBC5, 8 KiB battery-backed RAM; Game Boy Color required.
Keep your emulator's battery-save file when moving your game.

GAME BOY CONTROLS
D-pad: move or select.
A: choose or advance dialogue.
B: return from menus; A or B confirms the highlighted Yes/No answer.
A or B while exploring: open the field menu at a movement boundary.
Start: begin or use the menu/resume shortcut.
Select: save after the current movement step.

PYBOY KEYBOARD DEFAULTS
Arrow keys: D-pad. A: A button. S: B button.
Enter: Start. Backspace: Select.
